3. PROJECT SUMMARY

Design, development, and delivery of a web-based Quarry Management Application
for day-to-day operations across the above quarry sites — covering production
(block marking and load), parties, purchase and expense, HR and payroll, and
multi-user access with roles.

The application is quarry-wise (switch between Chithanavasal and Ariyur) and
supports Owner, Accountant, and Viewer roles.


4. SCOPE OF WORK  (included in this quotation)

4.1  Overview
     •  Dashboard — monthly summary, cash flow, category breakup, production
     •  All Transactions — unified debit / credit register with filters

4.2  Procurement & Sale
     •  Block Marking — batches, GST, party, payment, marking numbers
     •  Block Load / Dispatch — trips, vehicles, linked blocks
     •  Customer master and customer ledger / detail

4.3  Purchase & Expense
     •  Purchase & Expense vouchers
     •  Monthly Gift
     •  Royalty
     •  Machinery Rent
     •  Vendor master and vendor ledger / detail

4.4  HR & Payroll
     •  Staff Management
     •  Daily Attendance
     •  Salary Sheet

4.5  Setup & Access
     •  Masters — quarry setup and default GST
     •  Users & roles — Owner / Accountant / Viewer, quarry access
     •  Login and session security

4.6  Delivery
     •  Web application (desktop and mobile browser)
     •  Deployment to the live environment
     •  Basic user training for office staff
     •  Source handover as mutually agreed at go-live
